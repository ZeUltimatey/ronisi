# Ronīši 2026 content and language update

## Language switching

The accessibility language control now switches the public website between Latvian and English, including navigation, footer, homepage, news, preparation, about, organisers, gallery controls, applications, regulations, FAQ, contacts, schedule, map and results-table interface.

The selected language remains stored in `localStorage` under `ronisi-accessibility-settings-v1`.

## Regulations alignment

The public content and FAQ were aligned with the supplied 2026 regulations, including:

- event dates and registration times;
- maximum of 32 teams;
- team size and gender composition;
- application period;
- EUR 135 team fee and payment deadline;
- included breakfast, first aid and awards;
- roster-change deadline;
- optional Saturday and Sunday lunches;
- cottage booking dates and process;
- sports disciplines and required participant numbers;
- required identity and student documents;
- 3 additional points for a team flag.

Unsupported FAQ claims about swimming, tents, showers and unrestricted guest arrangements were removed.

## Google Form

The judge application is embedded directly in the website and can be completed and submitted inside the page. Both the full Google Forms URL and the supplied `forms.gle` short URL are supported. The website language setting adds the appropriate Google interface language parameter.

The questions inside a Google Form remain in the language in which the form owner authored them. To provide translated questions, add English translations in Google Forms itself or create a separate English form URL.
